# Content review: where facts are needed

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Suggestions only: no copy was changed. Search engines and AI answers quote concrete facts, such as names, places, numbers and notes, and skip vague lines. Each item below says what to replace and what kind of fact would do it. Anything marked **claim** should be confirmed or removed before launch, because AI answers may repeat it as fact.

## Fixed on 2026-10-14

- **Delivery & returns, Privacy policy and Terms** were rewritten to match what the site does. They now cover delivery within Kerala only, Razorpay, Resend and the courier, data rights under the DPDP Act, and Muhammed Yasir as proprietor and grievance officer. The client should still read them.
- **"100% Satisfaction Guarantee"** was replaced by "7-Day Returns" (the real policy), and **"Swift & Secure Delivery"** by the real delivery figures.
- **"organic"** and the **"holistic / clean beauty"** lines were removed from Our Story.

## Home page

- **Hero:** "Fine Fragrance For Modern You" / "Discover a fragrance that expresses your individuality." Add one concrete line, for example the concentration, where the perfumes are made, and free delivery across India.
- **Collection cards:** "Strength and refinement.", "Delicate power, from day to night.", "Made to be shared." Name the character of each collection instead (for example its main scent families or signature notes).
- **Film section:** "…our collections bridge the gap between tradition and modern luxury." Say which tradition (for example attar or oud perfumery) and how it shows in the perfumes.
- **"The house" band:** "A perfume is your invisible, unforgettable signature." This repeats the film section's first sentence. Use one, and replace the other with a fact about the brand (when and where it started, who makes the perfumes).

## Shop, collections and offers

- **All perfumes intro:** "Fine fragrances for modern you, each composed to be worn as a signature." Replace with facts: how many perfumes, their concentration, sizes, and the price range.
- **Collection intros** ("Strength and refinement, captured in a bottle. Discover the scent that speaks for you." and the others). Describe what is in each collection.
- **Offers intro:** "Our signature fragrances at a little less, while stocks last." State the offer (for example the discount and the end date) when there is one.

## Product pages

- **Ayyara One description:** currently a list of notes with spelling mistakes ("sandlwood", "jadmine"). Replace it with two or three sentences on what it smells like, who it is for and when to wear it, and move the notes into the notes fields.
- **Tagline and SEO title/description:** the backend supports them, but the admin has no fields for them yet, so they are always empty. Either add the fields, or rely on the generated title and description.
- **Fragrance profiles:** see `MISSING_PRODUCT_DATA.md`.

## Our Story (About)

- **"Where the earth's finest botanicals meet modern alchemy"** (meta description and copy). Name the actual key ingredients and where they come from.
- **"We harvest bright, living botanicals"** (claim). Does the brand harvest anything itself? If not, say who supplies the ingredients.
- **"We source from the most vibrant landscapes"** (claim). Name the regions or suppliers.
- **"Uncompromising Quality … only the finest premium ingredients"** (claim). Replace with a checkable fact (for example the fragrance house the oils come from, or the quality checks each batch gets).
- **Images:** the code notes these are concept images. Replace them with the brand's own photos, especially any showing an "Ayyara" bottle that isn't a real product.
- **New sections waiting for the client** (drafts, hidden on the live site): founder and perfumer bios, where the ingredients come from, and how each perfume is made.
