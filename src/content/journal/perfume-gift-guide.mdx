## How do I choose a perfume for someone else?

Start from what they already wear. A quick look at the bottles on their shelf, or a question to someone who knows them, tells you which scent families they like. Choose something in the same family rather than something completely new.

## What if I don’t know their taste?

Choose a unisex fragrance, or a widely liked fresh or lightly woody scent. These are the safest choices. A smaller size is also a thoughtful way to let someone try a new perfume.
