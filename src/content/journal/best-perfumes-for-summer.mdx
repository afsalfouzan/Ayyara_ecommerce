## Which perfumes are best for summer?

Lighter scent families tend to feel best in the heat: citrus, aquatic, green and fresh fragrances. Heavier amber, gourmand and dense woody scents can feel cloying when it is hot and humid, though a light touch of a woody base can still work well.

## Why does perfume smell stronger in the heat?

Warm skin and warm air make fragrance evaporate faster, so it projects more strongly but for a shorter time. That is why one or two sprays in summer can go as far as three or four in cooler weather.

## How should I wear perfume in summer?

- Use fewer sprays than usual, and reapply later if you want to.
- Apply to moisturised skin after a shower; lotion helps the scent hold in the heat.
- Avoid spraying where your skin will be in direct sun for long periods, as some fragrance ingredients can make skin more sensitive to sunlight.
- Keep the bottle out of hot cars and away from sunny windowsills.

## What about the monsoon?

Humidity holds scent close to the skin and can make heavy fragrances feel muggy. Fresh, green and lightly woody scents usually sit best in damp weather.
